# EGAT (กฟผ.) customer mix: direct customers and the energy-solution / C&I business

Research date: 2026-11-09. Thai Buddhist years (พ.ศ.) are converted to CE: 2567 = 2024, 2568 = 2025, 2569 = 2026.

## Q1. Share of EGAT electricity sales to PEA, MEA, direct customers and neighbouring countries

### Takeaway
EGAT is almost purely a wholesale seller. In 2024 (ปี 2567) it sold 196,795.37 GWh: about 71.5% to PEA, 27.1% to MEA, 0.47% to direct customers (ลูกค้าตรง), 0.72% to neighbouring countries (Laos, Malaysia, Cambodia) and 0.21% to standby/temporary/other customers. Monthly data for 2026 shows the same pattern, with direct customers at roughly 0.6–0.9% of volume.

### Cited Findings
- **FY2024 (ปี 2567), official EGAT figures:** total sales 196,795.37 million kWh (GWh). By buyer: MEA (กฟน.) 53,369.19 GWh; PEA (กฟภ.) 140,680.96 GWh; direct customers (ลูกค้าตรง) 930.46 GWh; neighbouring countries (สปป.ลาว, มาเลเซีย, กัมพูชา) 1,410.69 GWh; "standby/temporary power and others" (ลูกค้าประเภทไฟฟ้าสำรอง ไฟฟ้าชั่วคราว และอื่น ๆ) 404.06 GWh — [EGAT bond disclosure (ส่วนที่ 2.1 การประกอบธุรกิจและผลการดำเนินงาน), filed on ThaiBMA 29 Aug 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
  - Shares calculated from these figures (my arithmetic, not stated in the source): PEA 71.49%, MEA 27.12%, direct customers 0.47%, neighbouring countries 0.72%, standby/temporary/other 0.21%. The components add up to the total, so the arithmetic is consistent.
- **Context, 2024:** net energy generated and purchased by the EGAT system rose from 208,106.28 GWh (2023) to 220,574.88 GWh (2024), up 5.99%. Supply mix in 2024: natural gas 135,718.60 GWh (61.53%), imports 33,957.66 GWh (15.40%), coal/lignite 31,525.40 GWh (14.29%) — [same EGAT disclosure](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
- **September 2026 (ก.ย. 2569), latest monthly EGAT statistic:** total 18,241.33 GWh / 57,009.05 million baht. MEA 4,812.58 GWh (26.39%) / 14,722.19 MB (25.83%); PEA 13,271.16 GWh (72.25%) / 41,595.39 MB (72.96%); direct customers 112.83 GWh (0.62%) / 406.22 MB (0.71%); EDL (Laos) 7.72 GWh (0.04%) / 16.64 MB; TNB (Malaysia) 37.04 GWh (0.20%) / 268.61 MB (0.47%); Cambodia nil — [EGAT "การจำหน่ายพลังงานไฟฟ้า – กราฟล่าสุด"](https://www.egat.co.th/home/statistics-transaction-latest/)
  - Note: the listed percentages add to about 99.5%. The rest is probably a standby/other line not shown on the page, or rounding. Revenue per unit for direct customers (about 3.60 THB/kWh) is slightly above MEA (about 3.06) and PEA (about 3.13) in that month. This is my calculation from the table.
- An earlier view of the same EGAT statistics page (July 2026) showed direct-customer sales of 164.62 GWh, or 0.86% of the month's total. This comes from a search-result snippet only and was not re-verified on the page — [EGAT stats page](https://www.egat.co.th/home/statistics-transaction-latest/)
- EGAT links a full annual series (ปี 2512–2568) on its statistics section. I did not retrieve that page — [EGAT กราฟและสถิติ](https://www.egat.co.th/home/category/business-ops/stats/)

### Inferences
- In 2024, direct customers made up less than 0.5% of EGAT's sales volume. In practice, EGAT's "customer mix" means PEA plus MEA (about 98.6%). Industry-level demand in Thailand appears mainly in PEA/MEA retail sales data, not in EGAT data.
- Direct-customer revenue in 2024 is not broken out in the sources I read. At roughly 3.5–3.6 THB/kWh, 930 GWh would be about 3.3 billion THB. This is a rough estimate only, based on the Sept-2026 average price.

### Gaps
- I did not obtain EGAT's 2025 annual sales split (the 2025 annual report may be published by mid-2026). The latest full-year official split I found is 2024.
- I did not obtain revenue by customer group for full-year 2024 or 2025.
- There is no breakdown of the 1,410.69 GWh of 2024 neighbouring-country sales by country (Laos, Malaysia, Cambodia) in the source I read.

## Q2. Who EGAT's direct customers (ลูกค้าตรง) are, how many, which industries, how much they buy

### Takeaway
Direct customers are a small, legally restricted group: a few large industrial plants (cement, steel, petrochemical/refining, zinc), plus military/government sites and standby supply to IPPs/SPPs. EGAT does not publish an official named list, a customer count or an industry breakdown in the sources I found. The best available lists come from a 2018 law-firm guide and an unofficial analysis of EGAT budget files.

### Cited Findings
- EGAT's mission is to sell to MEA, PEA, "ผู้ใช้ไฟฟ้าตามที่กฎหมายกำหนด" (electricity users as prescribed by law), and neighbouring countries such as Laos and Malaysia. This is set under the EGAT Act B.E. 2511, last amended B.E. 2535 (1992) — [EGAT bond disclosure 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf); also [EGAT แผนแม่บทลูกค้าและตลาด 2568–2572](https://www.egat.co.th/sustainability/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/แผนแม่บทลูกค้าและตลาด-2568-2572.pdf)
- EGAT sells via its 500/230/132/115/69 kV grid to "ผู้ใช้ไฟฟ้าที่รับซื้อโดยตรงจาก กฟผ.", MEA and PEA. It sells to Laos at 115 kV and 22 kV and to Malaysia via 300 kV HVDC — [EGAT bond disclosure 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
- A legal guide (law stated as at 1 May 2018) says EGAT sells to "a number of direct customers prescribed by law, including Siam Cement Group (SCG), Chonlaprathan Cement Company Limited, and HMC Polymers" — [Tilleke & Gibbins / Thomson Reuters Practical Law, Electricity regulation in Thailand: overview (2018)](https://www.tilleke.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/PLC-Electricity-regulation-in-Thailand-overview.pdf)
- An **unofficial** analysis (author not named; "Electricity Profiles Cloudbook", R/bookdown) uses EGAT budget files from 2022 and lists 14 domestic direct-customer lines:
  - Sattahip Navy station
  - Voice of America radio station
  - SCG Tung Song (ทุ่งสง)
  - SCG Ta Luang (ท่าหลวง)
  - Jalaprathan (ชลประทาน) cement
  - Siam Iron and Steel
  - Padaeng Industry (zinc)
  - ESSO Ao Phai (refinery, Sriracha)
  - IPP-Ratch (Tha Rab)
  - IPP Ratchaburi Power
  - Others
  - IPP & SPP standby
  - Temporary
  - Thai Oil (refinery)

  It also lists 12 "neighbouring" lines (EDC, TNB, EDL plus Lao IPP plants buying back-feed). It says Sattahip Navy station is the largest domestic buyer, followed by SCG Tung Song and SCG Ta Luang. It reports total direct-customer purchases of 3,831 GWh (2010) falling to a low of 2,392 GWh (2023), forecast at 2,744 GWh by 2037 — [pchunark.github.io, "Chapter 10 EGAT direct customers"](https://pchunark.github.io/load_forecast_EGAT_strategies/egat-direct-customers.html)
  - **Conflict/caveat:** the 2,392 GWh figure for 2023 is far above EGAT's official 930.46 GWh for direct customers in 2024. The unofficial series probably includes standby/temporary supply, IPP station service and possibly neighbouring-country lines. It should not be used as "direct customer" volume without reconciliation. Some lines may also be out of date (e.g., Voice of America relay station; Padaeng zinc smelter status).
- In 2024, EGAT direct customers were eligible to buy the Utility Green Tariff (UGT1) by registering through egat.co.th. MEA and PEA customers register with their own utility — [ThaiPublica, Jan 2025](https://thaipublica.org/2025/01/3-electricity-authorities-launch-sale-of-2-billion-units-of-green-power/)

### Inferences
- By industry, the direct-customer set appears weighted toward cement (SCG Tung Song, SCG Ta Luang, Chonlaprathan), steel (Siam Iron and Steel), petrochemicals/refining (HMC Polymers, Thai Oil, ESSO), zinc (Padaeng), and government/military (Sattahip naval base). This is a qualitative inference from names only. **EGAT does not publish GWh or % by industry for direct customers, so no industry shares should be stated.**
- Total direct-customer volume in 2024 (930 GWh) is small. A single cement or steel plant may be a large share of it, but per-customer volumes are not public in the sources found.

### Gaps
- I found no official current count of direct customers (number of accounts) and no official named list for 2023–2026.
- I found no GWh or revenue by direct customer or by industry from any official EGAT/ERC/EPPO source.
- I did not obtain the text of the royal decree (พระราชกฤษฎีกา) that defines which users EGAT may sell to directly. A search confirmed the EGAT Act refers to "ผู้ใช้พลังงานไฟฟ้าตามที่กำหนด…", but the decree itself was not located.

## Q3. EGAT's energy-solution / C&I business (Smart Energy Solutions, ENZY, solar, BESS, EV, subsidiaries)

### Takeaway
EGAT has packaged C&I services as **EGAT Smart Energy Solutions**, covering:
- Solar PV (rooftop, carport, floating, farm)
- BESS
- The **ENZY** AI energy-management platform
- Design-procure-install services
- REC/carbon-credit services

It also has **EGAT EV Business Solutions**: EleX by EGAT, EleXA, BackEN EV and EV Total Solution. Named reference customers are mostly public bodies and banks, such as the Zoological Park Organization, Government Savings Bank (100 branches), Krungsri, MRTA, and IEAT/industrial estates via MOU. I found no counts, MW totals or industry shares for the energy-solution business.

### Cited Findings
- The EGAT Customer & Market Master Plan 2568–2572 (2025–2029) lists "EGAT Smart Energy Solutions" services for office buildings, industrial factories and businesses: Solar PV, BESS, Energy Management System platform, and design/procurement/installation/testing of PV and batteries. It also lists "EGAT EV Business Solutions" (EleX by EGAT stations, installation & maintenance for third parties, Elexa app, BackEN back-office, EV Total Solution, EV Expert training/certification) and a **Green Energy Trading** business under the Enhanced Single Buyer model. Strategic direction 5 targets services to IPS (Independent Power Supply / self-generators) and prosumers, VPP platform, EV charging for buses and trucks, ESS and battery value-chain studies — [EGAT แผนแม่บทลูกค้าและตลาด ปี พ.ศ. 2568–2572](https://www.egat.co.th/sustainability/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/แผนแม่บทลูกค้าและตลาด-2568-2572.pdf)
- **2024 activities:**
  - Launched EGAT Smart Energy Solutions with the Zoological Park Organization as a pilot energy-management model at Chiang Mai Zoo.
  - Installed solar PV at **100 Government Savings Bank (ออมสิน) branches** nationwide.
  - Signed an MOU with **Krungsri (Bank of Ayudhya)** to promote Smart Energy Solutions with knowledge and financing for businesses.
  - Held "Sustainable Industrial Future" seminars with the **Industrial Estate Authority of Thailand (กนอ./IEAT)**.

  Source: [EGAT bond disclosure 2025 (FY2024 operations)](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
- EGAT and IEAT signed a follow-on MOU for a "Smart City (energy and transport)" project, phase 2, extending to EEC industrial estates. A related page covers Samut Sakhon Industrial Estate. The signing date was not visible — [EGAT Energy Solutions news](https://energysolutions.egat.co.th/index.php/news/news-mou2); [Samut Sakhon estate item](https://energysolutions.egat.co.th/index.php/news/egat-supports-and-congratulates-the-director-of-the-industrial-estate)
- EGAT's English profile (2025) lists energy-solution partners including the **MRTA**, the **Government Savings Bank** and the **Thai Chamber of Commerce**. New private-sector services target "hotels, factories, restaurants, and energy users", and solar is installed for schools under a Smart Campus project — [EGAT Profile (EN)](https://www.egat.co.th/home/en/about-egat/)
- **ENZY:** an AI + IoT energy-management platform with a Monitor–Analyze–Control model. It connects to generation, BESS, air-conditioning, lighting and machinery. It produces automated reports and alerts, and EGAT claims up to 30% electricity-cost reduction. The 24 Dec 2025 article names no customers and gives no user counts. Offerings listed alongside it: Solar Rooftop/Farm/Floating/Carport, BESS, EV charging with BackEN EV, RECs and carbon-credit trading — [EGAT article, 24 Dec 2025](https://www.egat.co.th/home/20251224-art01/)
- A search summary of a Bangkok Post sponsored (PR) piece says **NER** uses ENZY for real-time tracking of factory electricity, including solar and biogas generation. I could not open the article to verify (redirect), and it is paid PR content — [Bangkok Post PR](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/pr/2646426/enzy-by-egat-reducing-electricity-costs-for-thai-industries)
- **EV (2024):** EleX by EGAT reached 289 stations, plus 100 stations via BackEN, for 389 in total. The 2025 target was more than 520 additional stations. EleX partners: PT gas stations, Saha Group, Home Product Center, BMW Thailand, Sathorn Thani building, Central Pattana. BackEN EV has about 100 main customers such as hotels, apartments, restaurants and department stores — [EGAT bond disclosure 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
- **EV (2025):** EleX by EGAT reached 305 stations, and the EleXA network covers more than 450 stations — [EGAT Profile (EN)](https://www.egat.co.th/home/en/about-egat/)
- **EGAT group companies (2024 shareholdings):**
  - RATCH 45% (subsidiary)
  - EGAT International (EGATi) 99.99%
  - EGAT Diamond Service (EDS) 45%
  - EGCO 25.41% (associate)
  - DCAP (District Cooling and Power at Suvarnabhumi Airport) 35% JV
  - **INNOPOWER 40% JV**: EGAT 40%, EGCO 30%, RATCH 30% per the disclosure. Business lines include grid modernization, Solar EPC and EV bus fleet.
  - InnoSpace 11.98%

  Source: [EGAT bond disclosure 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
- EGAT also provides engineering consulting for private solar farms and solar+BESS plants (2024) — [EGAT bond disclosure 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)

### Inferences
- Named Smart Energy Solutions references are mostly public-sector and financial-sector sites (zoo, GSB branches, MRTA, schools), plus industrial estates via IEAT MOUs. They are not large factory PPAs. The C&I business still looks early-stage and is project/EPC-led rather than a large recurring-revenue line.
- EGAT's subsidiaries and associates (RATCH, EGCO, INNOPOWER) are the more likely vehicles for commercial private-PPA work with factories. Their C&I client lists were not researched here.

### Gaps
- I found no revenue, MW installed, number of customers or industry split for EGAT Smart Energy Solutions or ENZY.
- I found no verified list of factory, hospital or university customers. The energysolutions.egat.co.th "Customers" page returned 404 and the homepage had no readable content.
- RATCH/EGCO/INNOPOWER C&I project lists were not covered.

## Q4. Published targets or data on Solar PPA, Solar+Battery or Battery services to industrial customers

### Takeaway
I found **no** published EGAT targets for Solar PPA, Solar+BESS or battery-as-a-service to industrial customers (MW, customer counts or segments). The only published service-related targets I found are for EV charging and green-power quota (UGT1).

### Cited Findings
- EV: target of more than 520 additional charging stations in 2025, after 389 total (EleX + BackEN) in 2024 — [EGAT bond disclosure 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
- **UGT1 (Utility Green Tariff 1):** launched 2 Jan 2025 by EGAT, MEA and PEA with the ERC.
  - Quota and price: 2,000 million kWh per year, with a premium of about 6 satang/kWh and bundled I-REC certificates.
  - Uptake: about 600 million kWh registered by 23 Jan 2025.
  - Main buyers: export-oriented firms with parent/partner emissions reporting and SET-listed companies, in banking/finance, manufacturing and department stores. PEA also reported interest from electronics, chemicals and petrochemicals.
  - EGAT's role: it serves its own direct customers and designed the "Arrangement Unit" matching RE output with usage on the UGT platform.
  - Next steps: UGT2 and Direct PPA were planned.

  Source: [ThaiPublica, Jan 2025](https://thaipublica.org/2025/01/3-electricity-authorities-launch-sale-of-2-billion-units-of-green-power/)
- EGAT's large-scale solar and solar+BESS procurement is **utility PPAs where EGAT is the buyer**, not services to industry. Example: GULF signed 12 projects totalling 644.8 MW (5 solar, 7 solar+BESS), 25-year PPAs, COD 2026–2029 — [InfoQuest 2023](https://www.infoquest.co.th/2023/360751)
- Industrial private solar PPAs in Thailand are generally private-to-private. Example: Amata B.Grimm with DENSO, 1.56 MWp floating solar, COD Dec 2026, with no EGAT role — [InfoQuest 2026](https://www.infoquest.co.th/2026/675259)

### Inferences
- EGAT's C&I solar/BESS offer appears to be EPC/consulting plus ENZY software, not EGAT-owned behind-the-meter PPAs. This fits EGAT's legal limits on direct retail sales (see Q5).

### Gaps
- I found no MW, MWh or customer-count data for EGAT solar rooftop/BESS installations at industrial customers.
- I found no 2026 targets for Smart Energy Solutions.

## Q5. Regulatory limits on EGAT selling directly to end users

### Takeaway
By law, EGAT may sell only to MEA, PEA, other utilities, "electricity users as prescribed" (a closed, decree-defined set of direct customers), and neighbouring countries. Retail supply to new factories is normally done by MEA or PEA. The ERC regulates EGAT under the Energy Industry Act B.E. 2550 (2007). New green-power channels (UGT, Direct PPA) are being opened through the existing Enhanced Single Buyer (ESB) structure.

### Cited Findings
- EGAT Act B.E. 2511 (amended 2535): EGAT produces, procures and supplies electricity to MEA, PEA, "ผู้ใช้ไฟฟ้าตามที่กฎหมายกำหนด" and neighbouring countries. The ERC regulates EGAT — [EGAT bond disclosure 2025](https://www.thaibma.or.th/Download/prospetus/4c48950b-74ce-4a51-b464-81299403d649_BASE/(2)120101_25680829.pdf)
- The EGAT master plan describes the industry moving from Enhanced Single Buyer to a Competitive Electricity Supply Industry, with the ERC regulating under the Energy Industry Act 2550 — [EGAT แผนแม่บทลูกค้าและตลาด 2568–2572](https://www.egat.co.th/sustainability/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/แผนแม่บทลูกค้าและตลาด-2568-2572.pdf)
- The 2018 legal overview says direct customers are "prescribed by law". MEA serves Bangkok, Nonthaburi and Samut Prakan, and PEA serves the remaining provinces — [Tilleke & Gibbins / Practical Law (2018)](https://www.tilleke.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/PLC-Electricity-regulation-in-Thailand-overview.pdf)
- A Council of State (สำนักงานคณะกรรมการกฤษฎีกา) document restates EGAT's objective as selling to MEA, PEA, other utilities by law, and "ผู้ใช้พลังงานไฟฟ้าตามที่กำหนด" (seen only as a search snippet) — [ocs.go.th document](https://www.ocs.go.th/ocs_website/file/reader/MDZjMDdmMDU0MDMzMDdlMDQ5MDZjMDdjMDdkMDRjMDY5MDdlMDdhMDU3MDZiMDM1MDdkMDRiMDZkMDc4MDUzMDU2MDUz)
- Direct PPA under NEPC (กพช.) resolution 1/2567 (2024) is discussed as a new channel — [InfoQuest "Power of The Act: Direct PPA"](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=410981). I did not read this article in full.

### Inferences
- Because EGAT cannot freely add retail end users, its route to C&I customers is mainly:
  - (a) services such as EPC, ENZY, EV and REC/UGT
  - (b) green-power products sold through MEA/PEA or to its legacy direct customers
  - (c) group companies (RATCH, EGCO, INNOPOWER)

  This explains why its direct-sales share stays below 1%.

### Gaps
- I did not find the royal decree text listing the eligible direct customers, or the date it was last updated.
- I found no ERC rule text on whether EGAT may take new direct customers today.
- I did not verify the Direct PPA (NEPC 1/2567) details, including eligibility, quota and EGAT's role.
